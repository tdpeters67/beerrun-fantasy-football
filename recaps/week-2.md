Redemption for **Ja'Marr Chase**, who followed up his 3.2-point no-show with a respectable **26.5**. Greasy needed it too, because not only did they forget to bench an injured Zay Flowers, but they also started Justin Herbert who had an absolute stinker. WTF is going on with the Chargers? **My Penix is Rising/In Tua We Trust** falls to **0-2** with a rough starting lineup. He matched a 0 point WR with a 0 point WR with a sluggish Marvin Harrison Jr. who looks like he's forgotten how to run routes. Meanwhile Denzel Boston (who??) and Bryce Young sat on the bench, both with huge numbers. Maybe next week this team can put their best players forward for the first W of the season.

### Etienne This D vs Dirty Danglers

**Etienne This D quietly moves to 2-0** and barely broke a sweat. Amon-Ra St. Brown went for **35.2**, Brandon Aubrey the **GOAT kicker** put up **17**, and they still stranded a league-high **68.78 on the bench**. For Eitenne This D, consistency will be king. **Dirty Danglers** finally got their receivers going — Olave (22.6) and Waddle (21.8) — but Colston Loveland handed them another **1.3 at tight end**, and that position remains this franchise's personal hell. Danglers is going to be hoping that their first round pick in Bijan steps up to his role if they're going to hope for a win.

### Ima Be Under Served Talent vs Sausage Party

I'm sure Ima B.U.S.T. characteristically busted over **Josh Allen's 40.82 points.** And although many players on the team had stinkers, Kyren Williams, Tyler Warren, and Harrison Butker (ofc) carried B.U.S.T to the **Blowout of the week with a margin of 41.** **Sausage Party** takes home **Biggest Loser** at **86.98** and drops to **0-2**. We'll find out next time if Sasuage Party can finally eclipse 100 points.

### William's Wild Team vs Cash Money 8 Ballers

**William's Wild Team gets their first W of the season** and did it the absolute hardest way by starting an injured **Puka Nacua**. Trey McBride (18.1) and Joe Burrow (16.2) had respectable games while Tre Tucker popped off on the bench (btw are the Raiders good??). This game really belongs to the ongoing tragedy that is **Cash Money 8 Ballers**, now **0-2 despite rostering Patrick Mahomes (28.98) AND Jahmyr Gibbs (23.3).** How do you lose with that? By insisting on starting a Titans WR over **Jalen Coker (14.6), Rachaad White (14.3), and Romeo Doubs (12.6)**. This was such a winnable game for Cash Money 8 Ballers. We'll see if they adjust their lineup for the next week.

### Bullpuckey Music for Idiots vs Ignorance is Vid's

**Nailbiter of the week with 0.64 points margin.** And oh, it stings. **Ignorance is Vid's** did have points on the bench that would have won the match-up. Vid's went from last week's luckiest winner to this week's most excruciating loser. **Bullpuckey Music for Idiots** who looked dead to rights for the season last week will gladly take it, powered by **CeeDee Lamb (35.3)** and **Jake Ferguson (20.3)** even though **Saquon Barkley gave them only 3** (although I think he was injured?).
