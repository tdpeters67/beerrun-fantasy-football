### Kenney Powers vs Greasy Hucksters

Game of the week match-up here. We got the Biggest Winner and the Most Unlucky of the week in an exciting head-to-head competition. Kenny Powers team was firing on all cylinders with very few missed points from the bench. Meanwhile Greasy Hucksters, who would have won against half of the league, did embarrassingly bench future hall of famer Mike Evans, electing to start TWO TIGHT ENDS for some reason. Lucky for Greasy, starting Evans wouldn't have been enough for a win. But if Chase actually had a half decent game, that mistake would have cost the game. End of the day, both of these teams look well developed and early shoe-ins for deep playoff runs.
