What's up my sons my name is Tomas and I am your commissioner this season of fantasy football. My plan is to do a little recap each week to keep things interesting throughout the season. In the future I'll try to be a bit quicker on getting it done by Thursday.
Anyways we had a pretty interesting first week of fantasy. We have a team whose QB scored negative points and still won, a first-round WR who did fuck all, and a bench that might've been able to beat half the league.

### Kenney Powers vs Greasy Hucksters

Highest score of the week, and Kenney Powers earned it: Jaxon Smith-Njigba (26.2) balled out and the whole roster showed up. Spare a thought for **Greasy Hucksters**, though — they dropped 130 points, a total that wins in most weeks, and got run off the field anyway. The kicker: they used the **8th overall pick on Ja'Marr Chase, who managed 3.2 points.** You scored 130 _without your best player._ That's either terrifying or heartbreaking, and this week it was heartbreaking. **Unluckiest loser of Week 1.**

### Etienne This D vs William's Wild Team

A 37-point beatdown and the second-highest score in the league. Amon-Ra St. Brown (28.7) did Amon-Ra things, and Etienne got contributions up and down the lineup. William's Wild Team put up a respectable 125 — a number that beats four other teams this week — and it still didn't matter even a little. Sometimes you draw the buzzsaw.

### Ima Be Under Served Talent vs Cash Money 8 Ballers

Justin Jefferson (31.2) and Josh Allen (**35.66, the top starter in the entire league**) is a rough combo to stare across at on a Sunday. **Cash Money 8 Ballers** have grievances to file, though: they nailed the **#1 overall pick on Jahmyr Gibbs (33.6)** and _still_ lost by 30 — because their **3rd-round tight end Brock Bowers put up a clean 0.0.** Zero. Nothing. A round-3 goose egg. Gibbs can't do it alone, fellas.

### Chimere Sit on this D=ke vs Bullpuckey Music for Idiots

**Blowout of the week (margin 51.6),** and yet Chimere is who this recap really wants to talk about. Kenneth Walker III went nuclear (34.1) and Chuba Hubbard (23.7) chipped in — but sitting on Chimere's **bench was Caleb Williams and his 37.26 points, the single highest score by anyone in the entire league.** You benched the week's best player and still won by 51. That's not a flex, that's a warning shot to the rest of the league. Meanwhile **Bullpuckey Music for Idiots** scored **68.8** — the low mark of the week by 34 points — and we may need to organize a search party.

### Ignorance is Vid's vs Sausage Party

Okay. Deep breath. **Ignorance is Vid's started Kyler Murray at quarterback and he scored NEGATIVE 0.38 points.** Your QB was a _net drain_ on the roster. On the bench, gathering dust: **Christian Watson (32.7) and DJ Moore (21)** — that's 90.1 points of bench, more than **Sausage Party's entire starting lineup managed (75).** And Vid's won anyway. **Luckiest winner of the week,** and honestly the luckiest man alive. Sausage Party, for the record, just got quietly outscored across the board — a clean, honest loss, no notes, better luck next week.

### Dirty Danglers vs In Tua We Trust

**Game of the week** — the only matchup decided by fewer than 30 points (margin 14). Two middling scores, but actual drama, and it came right down to the wire. Dirty Danglers survived despite **2nd-round pick A.J. Brown mustering just 5.6**, which tells you they had just enough everywhere else. In Tua We Trust will feel this one — a few more points from any bench spot and they steal it.
